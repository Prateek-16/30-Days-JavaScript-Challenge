A.P.J Abdul kalam

1931 - 2015

Missile Man of India

let's pay tribute to the former president of India, Bharat Ratna Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam. He was a scientist, a teacher, a president, and above all a great human being. He was born on 15th October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He was a great scientist and a teacher. He was the 11th president of India and served the country from 2002 to 2007. He was a great inspiration for the youth of the country.
